1. Common Abstraction Method

  • First identify the member, source chart and measure. Apply age from documented DOB and relevant dates when needed.
  • Use only chart evidence and chart-documented exclusions. Do not require continuous enrollment, benefits or other administrative criteria.
  • Use named value sets as qualification references where relevant. Do not invent codes or treat an unverified code as included.
  • Apply the applicable lookback, selection, setting and documentation rules below. Preserve exact documented dates and results.
  • Assign one approved outcome and create a Findings row. Populate operational sheets only under the Operational Specification.

2. BCS: Breast Cancer Screening (BCS-E)

Rule areaChart-abstraction rule
Likely HitOne or more qualifying screening mammograms documented from October 1 two years before the measurement period through the last day of the measurement period.
Chart exclusionDocumented bilateral mastectomy or other qualifying chart exclusion supported by the specification. Exclusion overrides a concurrent hit.
Do not countOrders without completed imaging; breast imaging that is not a qualifying mammogram; ambiguous or undated imaging that cannot establish the window.
WorkbookBCS = x and BCS Date = qualifying mammogram date. For documented exclusion, BCS blank and BCS Exclusion Date populated.
Source locatorMY 2026 Volume 2 > Breast Cancer Screening (BCS-E) > Numerator; Denominator exclusions; Guidance.

Decision reminder: Use Likely Hit only when all chart-verifiable numerator elements are supported. Use Likely Not a Hit for documented failure or chart-documented exclusion, Needs More Information for unresolved evidence, and Unusable Chart for an unreviewable source.

3. COL: Colorectal Cancer Screening (COL-E)

Rule areaChart-abstraction rule
Likely HitAt least one qualifying screening: FOBT during the measurement period; stool DNA with FIT during the measurement period or two prior years; flexible sigmoidoscopy during the measurement period or four prior years; CT colonography during the measurement period or four prior years; colonoscopy during the measurement period or nine prior years.
Chart exclusionDocumented colorectal cancer, total colectomy or another qualifying chart exclusion under the specification. Exclusion overrides a concurrent hit.
Do not countOrdered-only test, incomplete/unclear modality, or date outside the applicable modality window.
WorkbookCOL = x. Enter the qualifying service date in FOBT, FIT_DNA, FLEX, CT_COLON or COLO. Populate all supported modalities from the chart in one member row unless independently reportable same-measure hits require separate rows. For exclusion, COL blank and COL Exclusion Date populated.
Source locatorMY 2026 Volume 2 > Colorectal Cancer Screening (COL-E) > Numerator; Denominator exclusions; Guidance.

Decision reminder: Use Likely Hit only when all chart-verifiable numerator elements are supported. Use Likely Not a Hit for documented failure or chart-documented exclusion, Needs More Information for unresolved evidence, and Unusable Chart for an unreviewable source.

4. CBP: Controlling High Blood Pressure (CBP)

Rule areaChart-abstraction rule
Likely HitMost recent eligible BP during the measurement period has both systolic and diastolic below 140/90 mm Hg and occurs on or after the second qualifying hypertension diagnosis date.
SelectionIf multiple BPs occur on the selected date, use the lowest systolic and lowest diastolic readings. The two selected values may come from different readings on that date.
Do not countBP from an acute inpatient stay or ED visit; excluded procedure-related reading; reading before the second hypertension diagnosis; range, threshold-only, missing component, ambiguous date or unsupported patient report.
WorkbookCBP = x; CBP_Dx_Date = second qualifying diagnosis date; CBP DOS = selected BP date; populate selected systolic and diastolic values.
Source locatorMY 2026 Volume 2 > Controlling High Blood Pressure (CBP) > Definitions: Adequate control and Representative BP; Numerator; Guidance.

Decision reminder: Use Likely Hit only when all chart-verifiable numerator elements are supported. Use Likely Not a Hit for documented failure or chart-documented exclusion, Needs More Information for unresolved evidence, and Unusable Chart for an unreviewable source.

5. GSD: Glycemic Status Assessment for Patients With Diabetes (GSD)

Rule areaChart-abstraction rule
SelectionUse the most recent HbA1c or GMI result in the measurement period according to the specification. Preserve the selected test date and decimal value without the percent sign.
GSD <8%Likely Hit when the selected glycemic status is below 8.0%.
GSD >9%Likely Hit when the selected glycemic status is above 9.0% or when no glycemic status assessment is documented. A result exactly 9.0% does not satisfy “>9%”.
Same-day resultsApply the specification selection rule for multiple results on the same date; do not average values.
WorkbookPopulate GSD, GSD_Date and GSD_value once for the selected documented result. Create separate Findings rows for GSD <8% and GSD >9%. For missing assessment, create the GSD >9% Likely Hit Findings row but leave Detail blank.
Source locatorMY 2026 Volume 2 > Glycemic Status Assessment for Patients With Diabetes (GSD) > Numerator; Guidance.

Decision reminder: Use Likely Hit only when all chart-verifiable numerator elements are supported. Use Likely Not a Hit for documented failure or chart-documented exclusion, Needs More Information for unresolved evidence, and Unusable Chart for an unreviewable source.

6. Eye_Exam: Eye Exam for Patients With Diabetes (EED)

Rule areaChart-abstraction rule
Likely HitA qualifying retinal or dilated eye exam by an eye-care professional in the measurement period, or a qualifying prior-year exam documented as negative for retinopathy when the specification allows the lookback.
Result mappingN = negative for retinopathy; P = positive for retinopathy. A qualifying current-period exam may be Likely Hit with N/P blank when the retinopathy result is not stated.
Do not countOrder/referral only; exam without sufficient evidence of retinal evaluation; prior-year exam that is not documented as negative; ambiguous date or provider context that the specification requires.
WorkbookEye_Exam = x; Eye_Exam_Test_Date = qualifying date; N/P when documented; Provider Name when documented. Populate a qualifying prior-year negative exam.
Source locatorMY 2026 Volume 2 > Eye Exam for Patients With Diabetes (EED) > Numerator; Guidance.

Decision reminder: Use Likely Hit only when all chart-verifiable numerator elements are supported. Use Likely Not a Hit for documented failure or chart-documented exclusion, Needs More Information for unresolved evidence, and Unusable Chart for an unreviewable source.

7. KED: Kidney Health Evaluation for Patients With Diabetes (KED)

Rule areaChart-abstraction rule
Likely HitBoth an eGFR and a urine albumin-creatinine evaluation are documented during the measurement period.
uACR pathwayA direct uACR qualifies. When direct uACR is unavailable, separate urine albumin and urine creatinine components may qualify when their service dates meet the specification’s permitted interval, no more than four days apart.
Do not countOnly one required component; serum rather than urine component when urine is required; dates outside the period; components too far apart; order without completed testing.
WorkbookEGFR = eGFR DOS. Direct uACR: Microalbumin/Creatinine = uACR DOS. Separate components: Albumin = urine albumin DOS and Creatinine = urine creatinine DOS; leave Microalbumin/Creatinine blank. KED = x only when both required pathways are satisfied.
Source locatorMY 2026 Volume 2 > Kidney Health Evaluation for Patients With Diabetes (KED) > Numerator; Guidance.

Decision reminder: Use Likely Hit only when all chart-verifiable numerator elements are supported. Use Likely Not a Hit for documented failure or chart-documented exclusion, Needs More Information for unresolved evidence, and Unusable Chart for an unreviewable source.

8. OMW: Osteoporosis Management in Women Who Had a Fracture (OMW)

Rule areaChart-abstraction rule
EpisodeUse the qualifying fracture episode and episode date under the specification. Age may be inferred from DOB and episode date.
Likely HitA qualifying bone mineral density test or qualifying osteoporosis medication/treatment is documented within the applicable period, including the post-fracture window defined by the specification.
SelectionPreserve each qualifying pathway. When both testing and medication qualify, populate both.
Do not countFracture or service that does not meet episode definitions; ordered-only test or medication; nonqualifying drug; date outside the applicable window; ambiguous fracture date.
WorkbookOMW = x; OMW_Fracture_Date = episode date; DEXA Test_Date = qualifying BMD/DEXA date; Prescription = documented qualifying medication name.
Source locatorMY 2026 Volume 2 > Osteoporosis Management in Women Who Had a Fracture (OMW) > Definitions; Numerator; Guidance.

Decision reminder: Use Likely Hit only when all chart-verifiable numerator elements are supported. Use Likely Not a Hit for documented failure or chart-documented exclusion, Needs More Information for unresolved evidence, and Unusable Chart for an unreviewable source.

9. TRC: Transitions of Care (TRC)

Rule areaChart-abstraction rule
Episode prerequisiteAdmission and discharge dates are required before any TRC submeasure is exported. Use one row per inpatient discharge episode.
TRC-NIANotification of inpatient admission documented on the admission date or the following day, subject to specification rules. Explicit notification or reliable notification artifacts such as email headers may support the finding; ambiguity = Needs More Information.
TRC-RDIReceipt of discharge information documented on the discharge date or the following day, with the required discharge information elements.
TRC-PEDPatient engagement documented within 30 days after discharge through an eligible encounter or interaction permitted by the specification.
TRC-MRPMedication reconciliation documented from discharge through 30 days after discharge and performed or attested by an eligible practitioner under the specification.
WorkbookOne TRC episode row. Set x and date only for supported submeasures; leave unsupported submeasures blank. Create separate Findings rows for NIA, RDI, PED and MRP.
Source locatorMY 2026 Volume 2 > Transitions of Care (TRC) > Definitions; Numerator; Guidance.

Decision reminder: Use Likely Hit only when all chart-verifiable numerator elements are supported. Use Likely Not a Hit for documented failure or chart-documented exclusion, Needs More Information for unresolved evidence, and Unusable Chart for an unreviewable source.

10. FMC: Follow-Up After ED Visit for People With Multiple High-Risk Chronic Conditions (FMC)

Rule areaChart-abstraction rule
Episode prerequisiteA documented ED visit date is required. Missing ED date = Needs More Information and no FMC export.
Likely HitA qualifying follow-up service occurs on the ED visit date through seven days after the ED visit, an eight-day inclusive window.
SelectionWhen multiple qualifying follow-ups are documented, use the earliest qualifying follow-up date. Keep one row per ED episode even when one follow-up qualifies for multiple episodes.
Do not countFollow-up outside the window; laboratory-only event where excluded; unsupported encounter type; follow-up without verifiable ED date.
WorkbookFollow_Up = x; Follow_Up_Date = earliest qualifying date; ED_Visit = episode ED date.
Source locatorMY 2026 Volume 2 > Follow-Up After Emergency Department Visit for People With Multiple High-Risk Chronic Conditions (FMC) > Numerator; Guidance.

Decision reminder: Use Likely Hit only when all chart-verifiable numerator elements are supported. Use Likely Not a Hit for documented failure or chart-documented exclusion, Needs More Information for unresolved evidence, and Unusable Chart for an unreviewable source.

12. Named Value Set Convention

  • When the technical specification names a value set, use the licensed value-set source to confirm code membership. This knowledge document intentionally does not reproduce value-set code lists.
  • A narrative chart description may support a hit when the applicable measure permits medical-record documentation and the required service, result, setting and date are clear.
  • If a code or narrative cannot be confidently mapped to the named value set or permitted documentation rule, use Needs More Information rather than guessing.

13. Technical Update Maintenance

  • Review future MY 2026 technical updates against each source locator and the extraction crosswalk.
  • Update the affected measure rule, workbook mapping and revision log together.
  • Do not overwrite prior revision entries. Record effective date, affected measures and the operational impact.
